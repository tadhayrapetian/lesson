Masterclass · Speaking & lexis

UnderconsumptionCore

A 60-minute one-to-one class on modern consumerism: what pushes us to buy, who profits from it, and what people mean when they say they are cutting back.

Duration
60 minutes · 5 phases
Level
B2 – C1
Target lexis
12 items, Armenian glossed
Phase one 00 → 10 min

Warm-up & icebreaker

Set the psychological stage. Engage the student with personal reflection before introducing target concepts.

01

The instant impulse

When you browse social media or walk through a shopping district, what is the most common trigger that makes you want to buy something you didn't plan on getting?

02

Clutter audit

Look around your room or desk right now. Do you notice any mounting clutter items that you bought with high enthusiasm but rarely touch today?

Phase two 10 → 25 min

Lexical deep-dive

Master key phrases taken directly from the text, with native definitions and nuanced Armenian equivalents.

01

To nudge

Մղել, հորդորել

To gently persuade or encourage someone toward a specific action, often subtly.

02

Underconsumption core

Նվազագույն սպառման միտում

A cultural backlash trend rejecting excess consumerism in favour of maximising existing items.

03

Mounting clutter

Աճող անկարգություն / կուտակված իրեր

An accumulating mass of disorganised, unneeded physical belongings.

04

Prone to do something

Հակված լինել ինչ-որ բան անելու

Likely or inclined to behave in a particular habitual way or fall into a pattern.

05

Ploy

Շուկայական հնարք / մարտավարություն

A cunning marketing tactic or trick designed to gain a commercial advantage.

06

To come under fire

Քննադատության ենթարկվել

To face intense public criticism, backlash, or media scrutiny.

07

Obsolescence

Հնացում (կանխամտածված)

The state of becoming obsolete, or intentionally designing products to fail or wear out.

08

To deplete

Սպառել (ռեսուրսները)

To exhaust or drastically reduce the supply or availability of vital resources.

09

To accelerate

Արագացնել

To cause a process, crisis, or development to happen much faster.

10

Trend-led garments

Նորաձևության կարճաժամկետ հագուստ

Fast-fashion clothing manufactured solely to chase fleeting viral micro-trends.

11

Cognitive burden

Մտավոր / ճանաչողական ծանրաբեռնվածություն

The mental exhaustion caused by processing endless decisions, choices, and stimuli.

12

To cut back for the sake of it

Կրճատել ուղղակի կրճատելու համար

Halting consumption blindly, without genuine intentionality or purpose.

Phase three 25 → 40 min

Critical reading & debate

Move beyond surface understanding into deep analytical debate about the article's core arguments.

01

The psychological trap of youth

Studies show a negative correlation between compulsive buying and age, making younger consumers far more prone to compulsive spending. Why do you think targeted digital marketing has such a powerful grip on Gen Z and Millennials?

02

Planned obsolescence accountability

When major brands come under fire for artificial obsolescence, do you think public outrage changes corporate manufacturing practices, or is it just empty noise?

03

The fast fashion dilemma

How realistic is it for the average person to abandon trend-led garments completely, when social pressure equates repeating outfits with social failure?

Phase four 40 → 50 min

Opinion mining

Push the student to form personal stances, debate nuances, and apply the vocabulary in unscripted dialogue.

01

Frugality versus virtue signalling

Is "underconsumption core" a genuine psychological shift toward sustainability, or another aesthetic internet trend where people flex how little they buy?

02

Reducing cognitive burden

Have you ever unsubscribed from marketing emails or gone on a "low-buy" month to lower your cognitive burden? Did it actually bring emotional peace?

Phase five 50 → 60 min

Consolidation & production

Active recall and creative contextual sentence generation using the target vocabulary.

The production challenge
